# Client deployment checklist

## Commercial onboarding

- [ ] Legal business/store name, trading name, support contacts, billing entity
- [ ] Production domain/subdomains and DNS access
- [ ] Owned logo/favicon/social image and brand color approvals
- [ ] Enabled languages, default language, RTL review, currency and number conventions
- [ ] Catalog import source, categories, brands, variants, SKUs, stock thresholds, owned imagery
- [ ] Tax registration and rules reviewed by the client's adviser for each selling jurisdiction
- [ ] Shipping zones, rates, free-shipping rules, carriers, delivery promises, restricted destinations
- [ ] Payment merchant account, settlement currency, sandbox acceptance, live webhook and refund test
- [ ] Verified email sender/domain, SMTP credentials, reset/order notification acceptance
- [ ] Named first administrator and additional least-privilege staff roles

## Legal and customer experience

- [ ] Country-specific privacy notice and cookie/analytics consent requirements
- [ ] Terms of sale/service
- [ ] Returns, refunds, cancellation, warranty, delivery and complaint policies
- [ ] Business identity, tax display, accessibility statement and support details
- [ ] Product claims, age restrictions and regulated-goods requirements where applicable

No jurisdiction-specific text should be copied from another client. Obtain local legal review.

## Launch acceptance

- [ ] Desktop/mobile/RTL storefront acceptance
- [ ] Product, cart, shipping, tax, coupon, loyalty and stock acceptance
- [ ] Real low-value payment, webhook, invoice, cancellation/refund and reconciliation test
- [ ] Login, password reset, order emails and deliverability test
- [ ] Admin product/order/customer/inventory training
- [ ] Analytics/tag manager configured with consent controls where required
- [ ] External health monitoring and alert recipients configured
- [ ] PostgreSQL and MinIO backups scheduled, encrypted, off-server and restore-tested
- [ ] Hosting access, secret ownership, source/image licenses, runbooks and escalation contacts handed over
- [ ] Post-launch support window, maintenance owner, RPO/RTO and change-approval process agreed
